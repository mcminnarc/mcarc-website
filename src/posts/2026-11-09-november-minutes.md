# November 2026 Meeting Minutes

David did the pledge, Mike did the prayer.

**Roll call:** 21 members in attendance

## Officer Reports

**Treasurer Alicia Parham:** The treasurer's report was read and passed. 30 current members, 4 life members.

**Secretary Chan Patrick:** Corrections or additions, passed. Old motions: none.

**VP Bobby Murphy:** Thanked everyone for the great year we have had. Pumpkin Town went very well — 4 members helped with the trailer and brought it down, with the sole purpose of introducing people to ham radio, and there was a lot of interest. 30 people signed up for a new Tech class and the list was given to Mike Farmer. December 5th is the Old Fashioned Christmas in downtown Athens. Looking at Black Friday prices for possible radios for the next Hamfest; the budget for the 2026 Hamfest was $2,000. We may do our own food next year. A building with a lot of donated commercial equipment was discussed, as to whether we want to keep it or get rid of it — the equipment is out of date.

**Trustee Kevin Cornwell:** Talked to David Wricker — his climbing rate is $250, but he gives half off to ham radio clubs.

## Old Business

- **Chili Cook-Off:** The cook-off went well and there was more than enough. Mike won 1st, Deb won 2nd, and Bobby won 3rd. We made enough to cover the club's insurance for next year.
- **Christmas Parade:** December 7th. We will meet at the middle school and be there before 4:00–4:30 to help get everything set up. The trailer will not be there as it is not needed. The parade starts at 6:30. The rain date is December 14th, with the same schedule. The Grand Marshal goes first, followed by the dance studio, then it is first come, first served. We will use 060 and 090 simplex.
- **End of Year Dinner:** December 14th at 6pm at Mexiwing.
- **Club Shirts:** Shirts will be ready by next week.

## New Business

- **New Ham:** Susan talked about her sister getting into ham radio.
- **ACS Exercise:** Information was given for ACS for the upcoming exercises, including what to bring and the call sign that will be used. The exercise starts at 8, so please be there at 7:30.
- **Elections:** Chrystal Kern was nominated for Treasurer and accepted. Mike Elkins, Robert Murphy, and Chan Patrick were re-elected as President, Vice-President, and Secretary.

The meeting was adjourned.

Susan did the closing prayer.
